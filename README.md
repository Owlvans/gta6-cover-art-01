GRAND LEONIDA — Leonida Cover Studio turns your photos into GTA-style cast-mosaic cover art, entirely offline in your browser: no uploads, no network, your photos never leave your machine. The default cast is eight built-in photos — six distinct characters (the Driver, the Boss, the Siren, the Fixer, the Lookout, the Runner) plus a getaway convertible and a neon motel — arranged in thin gold frames on near-black under a sunset-striped chrome title, a neon script subtitle, role tags, a ★★★★★ quote strip and a billing block, all graded with one unified sunset look. Swap in your own photos, assign them panel by panel, pan and zoom each panel, edit the title and subtitle, pick from 4 grades, and export a finished PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts  # then open http://localhost:8080
```
