Leonida Cover Lab is an offline, multi-panel GTA-style cover maker that runs entirely from static files in your browser — no uploads, no network, your photos never leave your machine. Its signature move is whole-subject framing: every photo carries a focal rectangle around the person, and the renderer always fits that whole rectangle inside its panel over a blurred backdrop brewed from the same photo, so heads, torsos and legs are never sliced by a gutter. Lay photos out as a Poster, Duet, Triptych or Quad, pan and zoom each panel, type your own title and subtitle, apply a Vice Sunset, Neon Night or Palm Dusk grade, and export a finished PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts  # then open http://localhost:8080
```
